### About the Event

# ARIES Science Club | World Environment Day 2026

**Date:** 05 June, 2026

---

> World Environment Day is celebrated annually on 5 June to promote awareness and encourage action for the protection of the environment. It serves as a global platform to inspire individuals and communities to adopt sustainable practices and contribute to environmental conservation.

On this occasion, the **ARIES Science Club** organized a series of activities to promote environmental awareness and encourage active community participation as part of the World Environment Day 2026 celebrations.

---

## 🗓️ Schedule of Activities

### 1. Cleanliness Drive

* **Time:** 9:00 AM
* **Participants:** ARIES Research Scholars and ARIES Science Club members.
* **Core Activities:**
* Cleaning of the road stretch from ARIES to Hanumangarhi Temple.
* Cleaning of the Hanumangarhi Temple premises.
* Cleaning of the public park near the temple.
* Awareness generation on maintaining clean and sustainable surroundings.



### 2. Main Programme

* **Time:** 11:00 AM
* **Participants:** Around 60 students from Classes IX–XII from nearby schools.
* **Programme Highlights:**
* **Documentary Screening:** An environmental documentary by Sir David Attenborough.
* **Popular Talk:** Delivered by the Chief Guest, Vice-Chancellor of Kumaun University.
* **Colloquium:** Conducted by Prof. Yu Liya E.
* **Discussions:** Interactive sessions focused on environmental conservation, climate awareness, and sustainable development.



### 3. Photography Competition

* **Theme:** *"Environment Through Camera Lens: Himalayas, Climate, and Community"*
* **Key Focus Areas:**
* Encouraged participants to observe local environmental issues through photography.
* Highlighted the intricate relationship between the Himalayas, changing climate, and local communities.
* Promoted creative expression alongside environmental awareness.



### 4. Plantation Drive

* **Time:** Conclusion of the day's programme
* **Location:** ARIES Campus
* **Core Activities:**
* Active participation from students, faculty members, and ARIES Science Club members.
* Plantation of approximately **25 saplings** within the campus premises.
* Fostered a sense of environmental stewardship and sustainable practices among attendees.
