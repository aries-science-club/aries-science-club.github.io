# ARIES Photography Competition 2026: Environment Through the Camera Lens

Welcome to the ARIES Photography competition! We are inviting entries from Research Scholars, Post-Doctoral Fellows and Project Students to showcase their best captures under the theme:

> **“Environment Through Camera Lens: Himalayas, Climate, and Community.”**

## Submission Guidelines

* **Timeline:** Images must have been taken within the last year (2025 or 2026).
* **Geography:** All photographs must have been clicked within Uttarakhand.
* **Originality:** The submission must be entirely your own work.
* **Editing:** Basic editing (color correction, cropping, contrast) is permitted. AI-generated, AI-expanded, or heavily manipulated/composited images are strictly prohibited.
* **Requirement:** Each entry must include the exact date, location, and a short caption explaining the image's significance to the theme. (Keep it short)
* **Metadata:** Make sure your image file still has its EXIF data intact! We need this metadata to verify the date, location, and camera details.
* **Eligibility:** Competition open only for PhD scholars, PDF and Project students.

## The Creative Themes (Pick any vibe!)

*(Not limited to these only)*

* **Wild Biodiversity:** How plants and wildlife adapt to the rugged terrain, interact with their habitat, or show the delicate balance of the forest ecosystem.
* **Water & Ice:** The drama of melting glaciers, how water physically shapes the mountains, or the clever, hands-on hacks keeping mountain villages from going dry.
* **Fury & Resilience:** The reality of climate impacts (like landslides, forest fire or extreme weather) and how nature or people bounce back.
* **Mountain Life:** Traditional eco-wisdom, sustainable living, and humans in harmony with the hills.
* **Action & Awareness:** The beauty in small, impactful acts of conservation, be it community clean-ups, recycling, or local plantation drives.
* **Research & Climate Action:** The intersection of scientific observation, field research, and technological intervention in understanding and combating climate change in the Himalayas.

> "Avoid generic macro shots of insects or standard landscape snaps. We are looking for creative compositions that show interaction, climate impact, or environmental context!"
